Name: Vintage Bordeaux Theme
Summary: A refined Chrome theme in deep burgundy, soft ivory, and muted sage.

ENGLISH
Vintage Bordeaux Theme brings a classic wine-label feel to your browser. A deep burgundy frame carries a soft ivory active tab and toolbar, while muted sage marks the inactive window. Dark cherry text and icons keep every surface easy to read.

The palette pairs Bordeaux (#6D0808), Black Cherry (#2D0000), Muted Sage (#757D6F), Ivory (#EEEAD7), Light Ivory (#F8F6EE), and Warm White (#FFFDFA) for a calm, low-glare look with no wallpaper, no patterns, and no gradients.

• Deep burgundy frame with an ivory active tab and toolbar.
• Warm white address bar on a pale ivory new tab page.
• Solid colors only, with high-contrast dark cherry text and icons.
• No scripts, no tracking, no permission requests, and the new tab page is left untouched.

This theme only changes supported Chrome interface colors. It does not recolor websites or replace the new tab page. Actual appearance may vary with your Chrome version and system settings.
