Name: Vintage Bordeaux Theme
Summary: A refined Chrome theme in deep burgundy, soft ivory, and muted sage.

ENGLISH
Vintage Bordeaux Theme gives your browser a classic wine-label look: a deep burgundy frame, a soft ivory active tab and toolbar, and muted sage on the inactive window.

Dark cherry text and icons keep every surface easy to read, while solid colors with no wallpaper, patterns, or gradients keep the interface calm and low-glare.

The theme only changes supported Chrome interface colors. No scripts, no tracking, no permission requests, and your new tab page is left untouched.
